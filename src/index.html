      EcoCity

      Build to last

      Plan a city on a floating island, then watch it live for up to 500 years. Cities that burn through their resources or choke on pollution become dead planets. Your team is ranked on the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

      1. Foundations25 min · 10×10 land. Free to build; land, workers and water are not.
      2. Grow8 min · Land grows 1.5×. Spend your earnings on homes, clean energy and schools.
      3. Plan for the future10 min · Prepare for drought, famine and a changing population.

      How to play

      The goal

      Build a city that meets its people's needs today without using up what the future needs. After Level 3 your city runs on its own until it collapses or reaches the year limit. Your score is the average of 11 SDG scores across every simulated year.

      Building

      • Pick a building from the bar at the bottom, then click a block on the map.
      • Factories only go on their own deposit: coal factory on the 3-block coal deposit, gold factory on gold, the nuclear plant on the 4-block uranium deposit, mines on natural-resource blocks.
      • Every building needs water to construct and to run. The reservoir (water level) refills each year from the river.
      • Level 1 is free to build. From Level 2 you pay with the money your city earned.

      Workers

      60% of your people can work. Each building needs a minimum number of workers to run and works best at its maximum. When you run short, power stations get workers first, then food, water, services and industry.

      Between levels

      Level 1 → 2 jumps 8 years with a fixed birth and death rate. Level 2 → 3 jumps 50 years, and now your planning decides growth: hunger, thirst, pollution, crowding and unemployment shrink the population; food, homes, hospitals and jobs let it grow.

      A city dies when

      • its population reaches zero,
      • food or water stays short for 8 years in a row (overpopulation and resource depletion), or
      • pollution stays above 75% for 10 years in a row.

      Things that run out

      Coal lasts about 30 years, gold about 60, natural-resource mines about 80 and uranium about 300 at full output. Sun, wind, rivers and well-managed farms never run out.

      Scoring

      SDG 2 Zero Hunger, 3 Health, 4 Education, 6 Water, 7 Clean Energy, 8 Decent Work, 9 Infrastructure, 11 Sustainable Cities (homes, green space and how long the city lasted), 12 Responsible Consumption, 13 Climate Action and 15 Life on Land. Ties are broken by years survived.
