How to play

The goal

1,000 people live on your island: 300 children, 600 adults and 100 elderly. Build a city that keeps them, and their children's children, fed and safe until year 500. Your score is the average of 11 UN goals across every year your city lives.

Building

  • Pick a building from the bar at the bottom, then click a block on the map.
  • Coal, gold, uranium and mineral blocks are scattered. Click one to see how much is in it. A factory covers 1 or more connected blocks: choose the size and press Next path (or R) to change the shape.
  • Farms and other buildings may use spare resource blocks, but to mine there later you must demolish them first.
  • Level 1 is free. From Level 2 you pay with the money your city earned, and can upgrade factories (one more block) and homes (rooftop solar, rainwater harvesting).

People

Adults work; children eat but do not work; the elderly need care. Couples have 2 to 4 children: more when food and water are plentiful, fewer as schools and hospitals spread. People die of old age, and many more die of hunger, thirst, poverty (no work), slums (no homes) and polluted air. Hospitals help. Nobody moves to the island.

Rain

Rain comes from your city: forests and empty land bring it, pollution drives it away. Farms, dams and rivers depend on it.

Time

After Level 1, 8 years pass. After Level 2, 50 years pass with a stop every 10 years: something happens and you have 90 seconds to choose. After Level 3 the city runs alone to year 500 with five big moments. Every team faces the same four shocks (monsoon failure, heatwave, disease, a fuel price crash) in a different order.

A city dies when

  • nobody is left,
  • food or water stays short for 8 years in a row, or
  • pollution stays above 75% for 10 years in a row.

Reading your city

The numbers on the left are this year's reality. A number turns red only when something is failing right now. The Island Times reports what people are saying. Nobody will tell you what to build.
