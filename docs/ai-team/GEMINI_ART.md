# Prompt for Gemini Pro — art direction and illustrations

Paste everything below the line into Gemini. Attach two screenshots of the game as style references: `docs/screenshots/start-screen-1080p.jpg` and `docs/screenshots/level1-1080p.jpg`.

---

You are the art director and illustrator for **EcoCity: Build to Last**, a 3D city-building game about sustainability made by a college sustainability club in India for its tech festival (Technozion, 30 October). Teams of 2–5 friends share one laptop, plan a city on a floating island over three levels (about 40 minutes), and then watch it live or die over centuries. The two attached screenshots show the current look: a low-poly floating island with a river, forests, mountains and a waterfall, soft daylight, a navy-blue game HUD. We are moving the 3D city to professional low-poly models from Kenney (flat colours, chunky shapes, no textures), so the art must match that style.

## Part 1 — A short style guide (text)

Write a style guide of at most 250 words:
- the palette: 8 hex colours (sky, grass, water, earth, accent, warning, danger, UI navy), with one line each on where it is used;
- the illustration style rules (camera angle, lighting, level of detail, how people are drawn, mood);
- how the look should shift between a healthy city and a polluted or drought-hit one.

## Part 2 — 14 illustrations

Create these images. Every image must follow these rules:

- **Style:** low-poly 3D diorama, like a tiny model on a floating island chunk, isometric view from above at about 35°, soft warm daylight unless stated, flat colours, clean silhouettes, matching the attached screenshots and Kenney's low-poly kits.
- **Setting:** a small Indian town (neem and banyan trees, terraced houses with flat roofs, auto-rickshaws, paddy fields, saris and kurtas on the tiny people where people appear).
- **No text, letters, numbers, logos or UI in the image.**
- **Landscape 3:2**, at least 1200×800, plain soft sky-blue background (#CFE8F7) behind the floating chunk, so images sit cleanly on game cards.
- Keep the same island-chunk framing across all images, so they read as one set.
