# Prompt for Gemini Pro — screenshot review (use after each build)

Paste below the line into Gemini with the latest screenshots Claude sends (start screen, Level 1, a checkpoint event, a "what happened" card, the result screen; 1366×768 and 1920×1080).

---

You are a senior game UI/UX designer reviewing **EcoCity: Build to Last**, a low-poly 3D sustainability city-builder played by teams of 2–5 students on one laptop (1366×768 or 1920×1080) at a noisy college festival, for about 40 minutes. The benchmark is polished indie games like **Islanders**, **Townscaper** and **Mini Motorways**: beautiful, instantly readable, satisfying to click.

Review the attached screenshots. Be blunt and specific.

1. **First impression in 3 seconds:** what does the eye land on first, and is that right?
2. **Readability:** anything too small, low-contrast or crowded at 1366×768 from 1 metre away? Name the element.
3. **Consistency:** colours, fonts, corner radii, icon styles or spacing that don't match each other.
4. **The 3D city:** does it look like one coherent art style? What breaks it?
5. **Clarity of state:** can a new player tell what is going well and what is failing, without the game telling them what to build?
6. **Delight:** where would an animation, sound, or small detail make a click feel satisfying?

Then give a **prioritised list of at most 10 fixes**, each as: `[impact H/M/L] [effort H/M/L] element — what is wrong — exactly what to change (with sizes, colours or positions)`. Order by impact divided by effort. Do not suggest new gameplay features; only presentation.
